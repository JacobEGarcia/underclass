A note on how this magazine is made

Underclass is written and illustrated with the help of the same machines it covers. Every piece is directed, edited, and approved by a human editor. The illustrations are machine-generated; the judgment is not. Where a story relies on people who would lose work by talking to us, names and identifying details have been changed, and the story says so.

A note on the name

Underclass is named for the people the acceleration is being done to: below the algorithm, under the dashboard, at the bottom of every cost-saving diagram. The best possible outcome of this magazine's beat is that the name stops applying.
